About SV Groups

Simple ideas with immense depth

A prominent construction company since 1987, SV GROUPS has marked its presence in premium urban scales and has rooted itself as a reputed construction company — maintaining high standards by striking a balance between quality and finesse across many successfully executed residential and commercial projects.

Promoted by a team with more than three decades of experience in the realty business, we are passionate about harnessing innovation as a positive force for change in every element of project delivery.

Quality Management

Our QMS defines every activity, delivering a quality product at optimum cost and minimum impact on the environment. Every employee is QMS certified.

Project Review

A monthly review rates every ongoing project on progress, schedule, cost, quality, safety and housekeeping, with senior management present.
